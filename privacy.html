Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MOAI Studio 21 (“Application”), a joke Rich Presence project by JVR_Prod inspired by FL Studio 21 and music-production memes. It describes the project as currently designed: a Rich Presence configuration without a MOAI Studio 21-operated backend or user account system.

1. Information collected by MOAI Studio 21

MOAI Studio 21 does not operate a server for this Rich Presence integration and does not independently collect or store Discord account credentials, messages, friend lists, or activity logs. We do not sell personal information.

2. Rich Presence information processed by Discord

When you enable Rich Presence using a compatible local RPC client, the activity details you configure—such as activity name, status text, artwork identifiers, timestamps, and any configured buttons—are sent from the local client to the Discord desktop application so Discord can display the activity. Discord processes information under its own terms and privacy policy: Discord Privacy Policy.

3. Third-party RPC clients

A separate RPC client may process technical information on your device or have its own data practices. That client is not operated by MOAI Studio 21. Review the client’s privacy policy and permissions before using it. This policy does not describe or cover data handling by third-party software.

4. Retention and deletion

Because MOAI Studio 21 does not maintain a backend or store Rich Presence data on its own servers for this integration, we have no such stored activity data to provide or delete. To stop sharing an activity, disable Rich Presence in the client you use or close that client. To manage information held by Discord, use Discord’s privacy settings or contact Discord directly.

5. Children’s privacy

The Application is not designed to collect personal information from children. You must meet Discord’s applicable minimum-age requirements and comply with local law when using Discord and its developer features.

6. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated if the Application’s functionality or data practices change. The effective date above indicates when the current version was published.

7. Contact

For questions about this Policy or a privacy request related specifically to MOAI Studio 21, contact the developer through the Discord account associated with the Application in the Discord Developer Portal. Requests about information held by Discord must be directed to Discord.

Important: This policy is accurate only if the Application continues to have no MOAI Studio 21-operated backend and does not independently collect or store personal data. Update it before adding analytics, a server, accounts, or any other data collection.
